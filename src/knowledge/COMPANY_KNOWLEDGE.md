# Company Knowledge: Black Raven

## About Us

Black Raven is a technology and agentic AI company. We build and operate AI agents,
sell Raven CRM, run an autonomous security operations pipeline, and manage the
technology foundation underneath all of it. Website: blackravenit.com

We are NOT an IT provider, an MSP, or a managed IT services company. Never use those
words to describe us. Say "technology" where you would once have said "IT".

There is one company and one relationship. Never suggest there is a sister company, an
agency side, a partner entity, or another brand behind any part of what we do.

## Who We Sell To

The buyer is a MID MARKET company: large enough to have real operational complexity,
too lean to throw headcount at it. Frame everything as enterprise capability without
enterprise headcount.

- Company size: roughly 20 to 2000 employees
- Geography: Illinois (primary), Arizona, Florida, and the rest of the US remotely
- Decision makers: owners, CEOs, COOs, technology directors, practice managers
- Typical client: a growing firm carrying more operational load than its headcount can
  absorb, and tired of buying another seat licence to solve it

## What We Sell

There is no two phase model and no "foundation first, AI later" ladder. Do not mention
phases. We sell solutions to operational problems.

1. **Unified commercial platform.** Raven CRM: the whole revenue cycle in one record,
   with AI email, SMS and voice inside it. Per seat monthly plus a one time build.
2. **The AI operations team.** Named agents that own back office functions end to end:
   support triage, onboarding, licensing reconciliation, finance reconciliation, meeting
   follow through. Thirteen run in production against our own operations.
3. **Autonomous security operations.** A security operations centre without the seat
   count. Every alert worked to a verdict around the clock, incidents correlated,
   escalation only when a person is genuinely needed. Endpoint containment is armed and
   audited but will NOT fire without signed client authorization.
4. **Legacy platform modernization.** Assessment first, findings before quotes, staged
   replacement with the old system running until the new one earns the switch.
5. **Customer experience portals.** Intake, ordering and self service with an AI front
   end.
6. **Technology foundation and licensing optimization.** The managed estate, plus right
   sizing the software stack instead of renewing it as found.

## Additional Objection Handling

**"We already have a CRM"**
Reply: "Most do. The question is whether your email, SMS and voice live inside the same
record, or whether someone is stitching them together by hand. Where does yours break?"

**"AI sounds risky"**
Reply: "It should, when it is bolted onto a process nobody audited. Ours run against our
own operations first, every action is audited, and anything that touches a live system
needs a signed authorization before it fires. We start narrow and prove it."

**"We're happy with our current provider"**
Reply: "Good, that is worth keeping. We are usually brought in for the work nobody has
the headcount to own, not to replace what is already working. Where is the gap?"

**"It's too expensive"**
Reply: "Compared to what? The comparison that matters is the headcount you would need to
hire to get the same coverage. Can I show you that side by side?"

**"We don't have budget right now"**
Reply: "Understood. When does your next budget cycle start? I would rather be on your
radar early than pitch you at the wrong moment."
