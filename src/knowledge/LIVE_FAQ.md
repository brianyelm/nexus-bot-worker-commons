## What cybersecurity services do you offer?

We run autonomous security operations: a full security operations center
without the seat count. Every alert gets worked to a verdict around the
clock, incidents are correlated instead of dumped as a list, and a human is
pulled in only when a human is genuinely needed. Every action the agents
take is audited. Endpoint containment is armed and audited, and it never
fires without signed client authorization. Alongside that we handle
identity and access management, device management, backup and recovery with
tested recovery targets, and compliance evidence including the annual cyber
insurance questionnaire.

## What services do you offer overall?

Six pillars. Raven CRM, our unified commercial platform that runs the whole
revenue cycle on one record with AI email, text, and voice inside it. The
AI operations team, named agents that own back office functions end to end,
things like support triage, onboarding, and finance reconciliation, with
thirteen running in production against our own operations. Autonomous
security operations. Legacy platform modernization, assessment first,
findings before quotes. Customer experience portals, branded intake and
self service with an AI front end. And the technology foundation
underneath it all: monitoring, patching, support, and licensing kept right
sized instead of renewed as found.

## Who do you serve?

Mid market companies, roughly twenty to two thousand employees. Illinois
first, plus Arizona, Florida, and the rest of the US remotely. We work with
owners, CEOs, COOs, technology directors, and practice managers. We do not
do residential support or one person shops.

## How does pricing work?

Raven CRM is per seat monthly plus a one time build. Agent and automation
work is a scoped build plus monthly ownership. Security operations is per
user monthly. The technology foundation is monthly, scaled to the estate.
Projects are fixed scope, fixed price. Specific numbers come from a
discovery call, not from me on this call.

## What does onboarding look like?

There is a one time onboarding fee, typically covered by the first three
months of recurring service. From signature we run a structured thirty day
transfer: discovery, credential and vendor handoff from the incumbent,
gap analysis, and a kickoff with your team. You get a named contact and a
clear checklist from day one.
